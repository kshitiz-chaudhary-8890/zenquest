## Design direction (gpt-taste + impeccable applied)

`gpt-taste` pre-flight: Attention (arched-portrait editorial heroes),
Interest (asymmetric consultation pair, ruled service lists), Desire
(restrained reveals, hover arrows, zodiac/ray ornament), Action (shared
plum-tinted closing band). H1s held to ≤3 lines and ≤96px; no stamp badges,
no stats in hero, no meta-labels like SECTION 01. `impeccable` mode:
Persuade for landing/consultation surfaces; Operate clarity for service
conditions; Read structure for About/policies. Both skills available and
read; no skill use is claimed beyond this application. Palette derives from
the logo: brand purple `#803F9D` for actions, deep plum `#392044`, muted
lavender `#F0E9F4`, warm ivory `#F8F5EE`, charcoal `#30282B`. Type:
Cormorant Garamond editorial + Manrope body. Motion: hero entrance ≤0.6s,
single-unit section reveals (`once`, `clearProps`), `matchMedia`
desktop/mobile split, full reduced-motion support, no pinning/scrubbing/
hijack. Every section keeps its own composition; no repeated three-card
grids or identical 50/50 bands.
