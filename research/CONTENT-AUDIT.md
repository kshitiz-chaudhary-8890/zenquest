## Conflicts resolved

1. **Blueprint design:** the SOW and initial brief requested retaining the design. The later user update explicitly says the direction is wrong and requests a complete presentation redesign. The subsequent instruction is treated as authorization to change layout while preserving underlying facts and formats. The later message also repeats the earlier SOW preservation clause; the specific redesign request governs this presentation update. The original HTML, text and styles remain available for comparison.
2. **Experience/readings:** old Tarot copy says more than 14 years and millions of people. Neither is used as a verified fact. The requested 18+ years / 1,000+ readings remain visibly marked for confirmation.
3. **Question limits:** old Tarot text invites as many questions as desired. The preview uses the SOW's per-service limits and booked-time constraints.
4. **Reviews:** existing testimonials include accuracy and crystal claims. They were not republished; the preview has three clearly marked slots awaiting permission-cleared feedback.
5. **Claims and old services:** crystal offers, advisor booking, guaranteed outcomes, shopping policies, and horoscope feeds are excluded.
6. **Blueprint / astrology distinction:** original Blueprint content describes relationship guidance and Zoom sessions. It does not establish birth-chart compatibility as an inclusion. The redesigned page links separately to Two-Chart Guidance rather than adding that service to the Blueprint.
